## Context

The forces at play, written so a reader who was not here understands why this was
even a question. What constraints apply, what is being optimised for, what would
break if the wrong choice were made. State facts, not conclusions.

## Decision

What is chosen, in the active voice. One decision per record.

> We will use X for Y.
